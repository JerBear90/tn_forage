### ABOUT THE APP

**ForageFlow** is an offline-first mobile app (Progressive Web App) for mushroom, plant, tree, park, and trail discovery in **Tennessee**. It's a field companion — something you use on your phone while standing in the woods.

**Tagline:** "Discover. Identify. Explore safely."

**What it does:**
- Field Guide with 30+ mushroom species, 16+ plants, and 20+ trees with images, identification steps, and safety warnings
- Interactive map of 60+ Tennessee state parks and trails
- Fruiting forecast based on real weather data (temperature, rainfall, humidity)
- Mushroom calendar showing what's in season each month
- Foraging journal with GPS and weather tagging
- Spore print color scanner for identification help
- Safety beacon that alerts emergency contacts if you're out too long
- Trip planner for multi-stop park/trail routes
- Works completely offline after first visit
- Community sightings and foraging challenges

**Safety philosophy:** The app NEVER says anything is "safe to eat." All identifications are "possible matches only" requiring expert verification. Toxic lookalikes are always shown prominently.

**Target audience:** Outdoor enthusiasts, mushroom hunters, hikers, nature lovers, and amateur botanists in Tennessee. Ages 25-55, comfortable with smartphones, interested in foraging and nature identification.
